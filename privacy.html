Privacy Policy

Your privacy and data security are our top priorities.

Last Updated: August 09, 2026

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with a reliable and secure service platform, we collect the following types of information when you use Essential Lanka:

  • Personal Details: Your name, phone number, and profile picture.
  • Verification Data: For workers, National Identity Card (NIC) numbers and location coordinates to verify identity and build trust.
  • Location Data: Real-time location data (only when shared explicitly via our chat or job posting features) to connect clients with nearby professionals.
  • Communications: Chat messages, attachments, and call logs (metadata only) facilitated through our platform for dispute resolution and safety.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the data we collect solely to operate, maintain, and improve the Essential Lanka platform. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Match customers with the most suitable and nearby service professionals.
  • Facilitate secure communication (Messages and WebRTC calls) between users.
  • Verify the identity of professionals to ensure a safe environment for all users.
  • Process transactions, bids, and maintain a history of completed jobs.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your information is only shared in the following scenarios:

  • Between Users: When a customer posts a job or accepts a bid, limited profile details (Name, Profile Picture, and general location) are shared with the respective professional, and vice versa.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by Sri Lankan law, legal processes, or government requests, we may disclose necessary information.

4. Data Security

We implement strict technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Passwords are securely hashed using modern cryptographic algorithms (Bcrypt), and communication channels are secured.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information at any time through your Account Dashboard. If you wish to permanently delete your account and associated data from our systems, you can contact our support team.
