Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers about Essential Lanka.

How do I hire a worker on Essential Lanka?
Simply search for the service you need (e.g., Plumber, Electrician) along with your location on our homepage. Browse through verified profiles, compare their ratings, and connect with them instantly.
Are all workers on the platform verified?
Yes, 100%! Every service professional must submit their National Identity Card (NIC) and pass our strict quality and background screening processes before getting listed.
How do I pay the worker after the job is done?
You can pay cash directly to the worker upon successful completion of the service, or use our secure in-app digital escrow payment options.
How can I register as a skilled worker?
Click on the "Join as a Worker" button on our homepage or navigation menu, fill in your skill details and NIC information, and start receiving daily job alerts in your neighborhood.
What if I don't have a smartphone? Can I still use this?
Absolutely! You can register via SMS or use our special USSD menu by dialing **#777#** on any standard button phone to receive direct job alerts and updates.
